Privacy Policy — Crypt Crawl Idle

Last updated: 2026-09-06

Crypt Crawl Idle is published by Laggy Pixel Studio. It is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no servers of its own. This page describes the only data that leaves your device.

Data the game itself handles

Your save (progress, gear, gold, settings) and the timers that gate ad rewards are stored only on your device. The game never uploads them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Every ad is either a banner or a rewarded video you choose to watch. To serve and measure those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier and other device or app identifiers
  • ur IP address and approximate (city-level) location derived from it
  • vice and app information such as model, OS version, and app version
  • sic ad interaction data (an ad was shown, tapped, or completed)

The game requests non-personalized ads only. It does not ask for, and does not use, the Android advertising-tracking or iOS App Tracking Transparency permission for personalization.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its advertising policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your device's privacy settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If the data practices above change, this page is updated and the date at the top moves.
